#### Core Development & Rationale
The Assembly Studios Bandshell — dubbed 'the Pringle' by its design-build team — opened in Doraville, Georgia as the first and longest bending-active mass-timber gridshell ever constructed in North America, debuting for FIFA 2026 World Cup viewing events before converting to a permanent performance venue on the 120-acre Gray Media studio campus. The 4,000-square-foot, 82-foot-span saddle shell employs Chebyshev asymptotic geometry: elastically bent timber laths form square cells at the base and progressively rotate to diamond-oriented cells in the upper shell, creating the characteristic hyperbolic-paraboloid surface of constant negative Gaussian curvature. The doubly curved glulam ring beam — the most fabrication-intensive component, rationalized and shipped from StructureCraft's Vancouver facility — receives the lath grid and transfers loads to two precast concrete plinths. Form was derived through iterative computational models validated against 1:80, 1:50, and 1:10 physical models built in-house, making this a textbook case in integrating computational form-finding with traditional scale modeling for structural verification.

#### Key Industry Impacts
* **Engineering & Construction**: Chebyshev gridshell geometry allows complex doubly curved surfaces to be fabricated from straight, initially flat timber members using only elastic bending — no steam bending, CNC scarfing, or glulam curvature is required for the lath lattice itself — dramatically lowering fabrication cost and lead time for long-span curved roofs compared to alternative free-form timber systems. The ring-beam-to-plinth load path also demonstrates a viable hybrid approach (mass-timber superstructure on precast concrete substructure) that can be replicated for covered public spaces, transit pavilions, and outdoor performance venues in dense urban contexts.
* **Real Estate**: Long-span mass-timber canopy systems are increasingly being specified by developers as differentiating architectural features for mixed-use outdoor amenities, transit hubs, and cultural programming spaces — this project provides a concrete $10M cost anchor and compressed fabrication timeline (StructureCraft to Bailey install) for developers and their project managers benchmarking mass-timber pavilion feasibility.

#### Strategic Takeaway
The Assembly Studios Bandshell proves that Chebyshev bending-active gridshell technology is no longer confined to European research prototypes — it is now a commercially fabricated, permit-ready, storm-load-rated system that North American structural engineers and developers can specify with a real reference project, performance record, and supply chain in place.
